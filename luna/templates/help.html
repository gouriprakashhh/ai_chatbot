Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my cycle?

Go to Cycle Tracker and input the first day of your last period. Luna will automatically calculate your cycle day and predict your next period.

How do I log symptoms?

Navigate to Symptoms page and use the sliders and buttons to log your daily symptoms. You can track pain, energy, mood, and more.

Can I export my data?

Yes! Go to Settings → Account → Export Data to download your health records as a PDF or CSV file.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption to protect your data. Your information is never shared without your explicit consent.

How do I update my profile?

Click on "Edit Profile" in the sidebar to update your health information, cycle data, and preferences at any time.

Can I connect with other users?

Yes! Visit the Community Forum to share experiences, ask questions, and support others on their PCOS journey.
